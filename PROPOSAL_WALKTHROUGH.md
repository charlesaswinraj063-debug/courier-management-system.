## Spoken script

Hello. This is the proposed solution for a Courier Management System using Salesforce. The project is currently at the planning stage, and implementation has not started.

The proposed system will bring customer details, shipments, and delivery activities into one cloud-based application.

Its planned features are shipment booking, parcel tracking, delivery-agent assignment, shipment-status updates, and reports and dashboards.

The business objectives are to manage customers and shipments, track parcel movement, assign delivery agents, keep delivery status up to date, and provide useful analytics.

For example, the intended workflow begins by recording a shipment. A delivery agent is then assigned, progress is recorded through status updates, and the delivery outcome is included in reports.

The functional scope includes custom objects, relationships between records, automation using Salesforce Flows, and reports and dashboards.

Implementation is planned in five stages: object and field setup, relationship building, Flow automation, security, and reports and dashboards.

The exact fields, status values, assignment rules, and user permissions will be finalized against the course requirements. After implementation, the shipment journey will be tested and a working-application demonstration can be recorded.

This walkthrough explains the proposed solution only. Thank you.
